programdemos/mhc-compared/mhc-compared.xtl

mHC compared: the plain skip, Hyper-Connections (HC) and mHC (Xie and others, arXiv:2512.24880) side by side at equal depth, width and steps. Two parts. At the start, analytical: HC's H_res (the exponential of the logits 2 I - 1) and mHC's (its Sinkhorn-Knopp projection, 20 rounds), their products across 12, 24 and 48 layers, and both gain measures of each product. Then the nine runs of training-at-depth (its records, copied into data/, a row a step of the loss, the gradient size, the composite's forward and backward gains, its proxy, and 1 for a NaN or infinite step), each reduced to the figures the comparison needs; the Python re-implementation's figures (mHC-poc, docs/results.md, quoted in data/python-*.txt) set beside them; and the count of numbers each scheme trains. Nothing is trained here. The lines that mix, project and measure are written out (FM13 of X_eTaL-ML is the ask, docs/xetal-ML-asks.md).
