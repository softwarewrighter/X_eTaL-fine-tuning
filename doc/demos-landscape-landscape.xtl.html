programdemos/landscape/landscape.xtl

The landscape: the accounting of the ways to change a layer, each figure analytical (it follows from the method's definition, no training is run). For two layers, this course's 8 x 8 at rank 4 and one real layer, 4096 x 4096 at rank 16: for each family whose cost is a count, the numbers trained, the numbers held while training beyond the weights themselves (the gradient and Adam's two averages, where there are any), and the numbers stored afterward when the change is kept apart from W. Then the bytes of the layer itself at 16 bits and at 4 bits (QLoRA's base), and the adapter's share of the full count.
