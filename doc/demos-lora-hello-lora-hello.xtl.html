LoRA hello: what does a small adapter add to a layer whose weights do not change? The question behind it: how can a model learn something new without every one of its numbers being retrained (the cost) or overwritten (the forgetting)? LoRA's answer is to freeze the weights and train a small correction beside them. Here is one layer of that, shrunk to 4 rows and 4 columns so every number can be read: a frozen table W and an adapter of rank 1, with X_eTaL-ML's LoRA library. Nothing is trained here (the adapter's numbers are made up): this shows what an adapter is, where it sits, what it adds and what it costs. The layer is y = W x + s B (A x): W stays as it is, and only A (1 row of 4) and B (1 column of 4) would be trained.
