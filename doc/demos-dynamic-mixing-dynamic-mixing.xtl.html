programdemos/dynamic-mixing/dynamic-mixing.xtl

Dynamic mixing: the paper's own mixing tables (Xie and others, arXiv:2512.24880, eq. 7 to 9), made afresh for every input. The stream state X (S = 4 streams of C = 8) is flattened to one list of 32 and scaled to a root-mean-square of 1 (RMS normalization); one projection phi (32 x 24, the paper's fused form, its eq. 14) gives 4 + 4 + 16 numbers; each group is multiplied by a gating factor alpha (the paper starts them at 0.01) and a bias is added; then H_pre = sigmoid, H_post = 2 sigmoid, H_res = Sinkhorn-Knopp of the exponential, 20 rounds. The layer is the paper's eq. 3: the block's input is H_pre X (a weighted sum of the streams), and the new state is H_res X + H_post' f. Part 1: two inputs, their tables, at the gating factor set; the stages of the first input are part 1's printout, which the page steps through. Part 3: the gradient of a probe of the new state by alpha, phi, the biases and the input, written out (through the Sinkhorn rounds, the sigmoids, the projection and the normalization) and checked against central differences. Written out here: ask FM13 of X_eTaL-ML (hc:d_ynamic).
