programdemos/gain-by-depth/gain-by-depth.xtl

The gain by depth: what a mixing table does when it is applied at every one of 48 layers. HC's carried-state table, the exponential of the logits 2 I - 1 (moved by a shift c, a setting), and mHC's, its Sinkhorn-Knopp projection (Xie and others, arXiv:2512.24880, eq. 9), each multiplied by itself once per layer; after every layer the product is measured two ways, each named: the paper's Amax Gain Magnitude (section 3.1: the largest absolute row sum, the forward gain, and the largest absolute column sum, the backward gain) and the Python re-implementation's proxy (mHC-poc: log10 of the largest absolute entry). Analytical: no training, the matrices alone. The loops are written out (ask FM13 of X_eTaL-ML, docs/xetal-ML-asks.md: hc:s_inkhorn, hc:a_maxGain, hc:g_ainProxy).
