programdemos/training-at-depth/training-at-depth.xtl

Training at depth: the plain skip, Hyper-Connections (HC) and mHC (Xie and others, arXiv:2512.24880) trained on one tiny task, with Adam, from the same start. The task is the Python re-implementation's (mHC-poc), made tiny: each of 16 tokens, its successor the target (token + 1, 16 wrapping round to 1). A token is looked up in an embedding (16 x 8), sent through L layers, the streams averaged and scaled to a size of 1 (RMS), and turned into 16 scores by an unembedding (8 x 16): softmax and cross-entropy. Each layer is lesson D1's: H_pre mixes the S streams, their average goes through a block 8 -> 16 -> 8 with GELU, H_post hands its output back, H_res mixes the carried state. Every weight is trained: the blocks, the embeddings, and each layer's three tables of logits. The scheme is two settings: S = 1 and the projection on is the plain skip (every table is the number 1); S = 4 with H_res the exponential of its logits is HC; with its Sinkhorn projection, mHC. The gradient is written out, layer by layer, the Sinkhorn rounds included, and checked against central differences in the same program; FM13 of X_eTaL-ML (hc:l_ayer, hc:b_ackTop, hc:b_ackBottom, hc:s_inkhornBack) is the ask (docs/xetal-ML-asks.md).
