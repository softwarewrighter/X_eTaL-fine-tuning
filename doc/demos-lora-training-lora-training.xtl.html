programdemos/lora-training/lora-training.xtl

LoRA training: where the numbers of A and B come from. A frozen 8 x 8 layer W; examples (inputs with the outputs wanted) made by a hidden shift of it, W + T; an adapter that starts as LoRA does, B at 0 and A seeded. One step of training as a pipeline, stage by stage: the prediction, the error (which is the loss's gradient at the output), the gradients of A and B, the update; checked against the library's own step, W untouched. Then training by Adam with a record: the loss on the examples trained on and on examples held out, every few steps, and the adapter at each record, so a page can step back without training again.

One step of training

step : (Float, Float, Float, Float)

value · line 72

One step of gradient descent on the n examples trained on, a stage at a time. The prediction p (the layer as it is: W x plus the correction, 0 at the start); the error g = p - y, which is also the loss's gradient at the output (the loss is half the sum of the squared errors, lo:l_oss); the error carried back through B, b = B' g; the gradients of A and B, each a sum over the examples; the update, A and B moved against their gradients by the learning rate. W is not in the update: the step gives it back as it was, as the adapter after the step, (W2, A3, B3, s2).
