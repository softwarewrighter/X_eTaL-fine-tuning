programdemos/rank-and-scaling/rank-and-scaling.xtl

Rank and scaling: the adapter's rank swept from 1 to 8 on the learning task of lesson 3 (a frozen 8 x 8 layer, examples from a hidden shift of it, 8 trained on and 4 held out), under two rules for the strength s: LoRA's alpha / r (lo:s_caling) and rsLoRA's alpha / sqrt r (lo:s_calingRs). For each rank and rule, after the same number of Adam steps from the same kind of start: the numbers trained (analytical), the strength (analytical), the loss per example on the examples trained on and on the held-out ones (measured), and the size of the adapter's table s B A (measured: the square root of the sum of its squares).
