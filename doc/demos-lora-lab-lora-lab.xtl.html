programdemos/lora-lab/lora-lab.xtl

LoRA lab: how an adapter's correction is worked out, number by number, and what its rank changes. A frozen table W of 8 rows and 8 columns, and an adapter of rank r beside it: A (r rows of 8) and B (8 rows of r). The layer is y = W x + s B (A x), written here as a pipeline of four stages so that every stage's numbers can be shown: the input x; the squeeze h = A x (r numbers); the spread u = B h (8 numbers); and the output y = W x + s u. The layer is the one of X_eTaL-ML's LoRA explorer (its W, A, B and x, made up by fixed ripples). Nothing is trained here.
