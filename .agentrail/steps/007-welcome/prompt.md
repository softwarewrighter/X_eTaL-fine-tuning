Step welcome (inserted 2026-11-05 on the user's review of the shell
gallery: "what is the purpose of the shell-gallery? why does the demo
page not have a link to it? The stepper does not have any animations.
Gallery should greet beginners").

The gallery was built as a page for reviewing the shell's parts and
kept out of the catalog. Make it what a beginner meets first.

1. demos/welcome (renamed from shell-gallery; "shell" is this
   repository's word, not a reader's): it greets a newcomer and shows,
   on one small calculation, how the lessons work and how to use each
   thing they will meet: words that open, a question first, a
   calculation stepped through, pictures of the numbers, a sum written
   out, a change in words, how much to show. Written for new eyes and
   given its cold reading.
2. Listed first in the catalog (a "start here" track), and linked from
   the catalog's introduction and from LoRA hello.
3. The stepper animates: the numbers a stage works out appear one
   after another, what it reads from is shown moving into it, the
   sentence for the stage changes with it, a bar shows how far along
   the calculation is and, while playing, how long until the next
   stage. Motion is off for a reader who asks their system for less.
   Animation shows the recorded values: it changes none.
4. The numbers are shown in the cells of the stage pictures, not only
   as colors.
5. Tests, baselines, README, the plan, CHANGES.md; publish, check-live.
